About

Thirteen years, one Garching yard, 140 finished projects.

Berger & Söhne started in 2012 with two engineers, a pickup and a materials yard on Industriestraße. Today we run our own labour camp, plant and a 60-strong site team — and we still answer the phone ourselves.

We are a registered Meisterbetrieb with the Handwerkskammer München, ISO 9001 certified and insured for every site we enter.
