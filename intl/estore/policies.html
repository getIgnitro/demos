Policies

Shipping

Orders placed before 14:00 CET ship the same working day. Belgium 1 day, EU 2–3 days, UK and US 3–5 days. Free delivery on orders over $60; otherwise $6. Summer orders are cold-packed at no charge.

Returns

Unopened goods may be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund. Made-to-order pastries and personalised gifts are excluded. Damaged in transit? Send a photo within 48 hours and we replace it.

Privacy

We store your name, address and order history to fulfil orders and, if you opt in, to send one newsletter a month. Card details are processed by Stripe or PayPal and never stored by us. You can request deletion at any time at hello@maisoncacao.be.

Cookies

Essential cookies run the cart and checkout. Analytics cookies are set only if you accept them in the banner.

Terms

  • Prices include VAT where applicable.
  • Allergens: all products are made in an atelier that handles nuts, milk, gluten, soy and eggs.
  • Corporate and wedding orders require 48 hours' notice and a 50% deposit.
